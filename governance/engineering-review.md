# Independent Engineering Review Protocol

Version: 1

Review the exact proposed change against its current target branch, requirements, tests, ownership boundaries, and integration conflicts. Evaluate correctness, maintainability, compatibility, regression risks, and the authenticity and freshness of cited test evidence.

A reviewer must not use implementation-session self-assessment as independent evidence. Record the reviewed revision, observed evidence, blocking findings, unresolved limitations, and a technical disposition. A technical clearance does not substitute for a platform approval, a required test, or a merge authorization.

If changes or verification inputs become stale, repeat the affected checks. Do not turn missing, skipped, unknown, or failed verification into success.
